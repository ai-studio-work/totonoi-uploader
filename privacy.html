TOTONOI Uploader — Privacy Policy

TOTONOI Uploader is a personal tool used solely by the owner of the TOTONOI CHILL YouTube channel to upload and manage videos on that channel.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails, and manage video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, and translations) on the owner's own channel, at the owner's direction.

Data use

The app does not collect, store, share, or sell personal information. It does not transfer any data to third parties. OAuth credentials used by the app are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are used only for the uploads described above.
